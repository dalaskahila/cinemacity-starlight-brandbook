08 / CLEAR SPACE & SCALE

Room to be recognised.

Keep surrounding text, image edges and graphic elements outside the clear-space area.

Clear space

H = full wordmark height. Allow at least 0.5 H on every side.

Wordmark width

Starting test size: 35 mm print / 140 px digital. Increase when detail is lost.

Emblem height

Starting test size: 18 mm print / 72 px digital. Preserve the fine lines.

Working rules: validate sizes on the final surface.
